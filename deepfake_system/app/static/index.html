01

Deepfakes Are Getting Harder to Spot by Eye

Face swaps, reenactment and synthetic voices now pass casual inspection. Here's what this tool actually checks, and what it's honest about not covering.

What it does

Isolates every face in a video, scores each frame for manipulation, and returns a hash-sealed report you can download, share or keep on file as evidence.

What it checks

  • Face-level artefacts across compressed, re-shared video.
  • Spatial, temporal and spectral cues fused into one score.
  • A confidence margin, not just a flat "fake" or "real".
  • Audio–visual lip-sync and synthetic-voice indicators.

What's covered

  • Covered: face swap, reenactment and synthesis in short clips.
  • Covered: WhatsApp / Telegram re-encoded footage.
  • Not covered: full-body synthesis, still images, live streams.

What you get back

  • A clear verdict with a fake-probability score.
  • Per-frame face crops and a plain-English explanation.
  • A downloadable, SHA-256-sealed PDF report.
  • A private history of every case you've run.

How It Works

Four steps from an unverified clip to a report you can trust and keep.

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Create a free account. This keeps every scan private to you and gives you a history you can come back to any time you log in.

  2. 2

    Upload the video

    Drag a file into the panel, or press Run a Sample Clip if you just want to see it in action. MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and WebM up to 250 MB.

  3. 3

    Read the verdict

    You get a score, a confidence band around it, the per-frame profile, the face crops used, and a plain-English note explaining what drove the result.

  4. 4

    Save the report

    Download a PDF holding the verdict, the file hash and the signal breakdown. Every case stays in your history, so you can reopen and re-export it anytime you're signed in.

Your video is held in memory for the duration of the scan and discarded immediately afterwards. It's never written to disk, never kept. Only the written findings and the file hash are saved to your history, so the tool is safe to use on sensitive material.

          FAQ

          Common Questions

          The things people usually want to know before uploading a video.

          Is my video stored anywhere?

          No. Your video is held in memory only for the duration of the scan and deleted immediately afterwards. It is never written to disk and never added to your history. Only the written findings and a SHA-256 hash of the file are saved, so we can show you the case later without keeping a copy of the media itself.

          What video formats and sizes are supported?

          MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, 3GP and M4V, up to 250 MB per file. If your file is larger, trim the clip to the part you want checked.

          How accurate is the detection?

          98.2% balanced accuracy on 200 held-out clips the model never saw during training or threshold selection (see Accuracy You Can Verify). Footage very different from those test sets (new generators, unusual lighting, heavy compression) can score lower, which is why every result comes with a confidence margin instead of a bare yes or no.

          Why did I get "Inconclusive" instead of a clear answer?

          The score's confidence margin straddled the decision line, so calling it either way would be a guess. Rather than force a verdict it isn't sure about, the system tells you it needs a closer look. This is usually caused by heavy re-compression (e.g. a messenger re-upload) or a face that's only partly visible.

          What if no face is found in my video?

          Face-level analysis needs a visible face to examine. If none is detected (heavy occlusion, an extreme angle, or very low resolution), the system reports "no face found" rather than guessing at a score.

          Do I need an account to use this?

          Yes, a free account. It keeps your scans private to you and gives you a history you can reopen and re-export as a PDF any time you sign back in.

          Can I use a report as legal or forensic proof?

          Treat it as an investigative indicator, not a legal determination on its own. A score above the threshold means manipulation signatures were found, not that manipulation is proven, and corroboration is still required.
