        RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

        Forensic Detection Architecture

        Constructed in accordance with academic specifications: MTCNN face tracking, frequency domain analysis, and zero-retention privacy.

        MTCNN Facial Alignment

        Detects and normalizes facial regions across video frames. Extracted crops are standardized to 224×224 for facial landmark and boundary consistency inspection.

        2D-FFT Spectral Profiling

        Computes 2D Fast Fourier Transforms across face regions to isolate checkerboard and upsampling artifacts inherent to generative convolutional synthesis models.

        Temporal Motion Coherence

        Monitors inter-frame optical flow vectors and boundary stability across sequential frames to detect temporal flickering and unnatural landmark variance.

        Zero-Retention Privacy (ISO 27037)

        Submitted media is buffered exclusively in temporary RAM for feature extraction and immediately unlinked upon scoring, ensuring zero disk retention.
